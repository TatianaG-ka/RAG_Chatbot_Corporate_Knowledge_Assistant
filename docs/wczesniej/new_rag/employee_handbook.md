# Acme Corp — Employee Handbook

**Version 4.2** · Effective date: January 1, 2026 · Owner: People Operations

---

## 1. Welcome and purpose of this handbook

This handbook describes the policies, expectations, and benefits that apply to all full-time and part-time employees of Acme Corp. It supersedes all previous handbook versions. For role-specific addenda (engineering on-call, sales commission plans, customer support shift premiums) refer to the relevant team manual.

Where this handbook conflicts with a written employment contract, the contract takes precedence. Where it conflicts with applicable law (national or EU level), the law takes precedence.

---

## 2. Working hours and flexible work

### 2.1 Standard working hours

Standard working hours are 40 hours per week, distributed Monday through Friday. Core hours are 10:00–16:00 in the employee's home timezone — during this window employees are expected to be reachable for synchronous communication (Slack, calls, ad-hoc meetings).

Outside core hours, employees can shift their work to fit personal commitments. Time tracking is not required for salaried roles, but quarterly check-ins with the manager review actual working patterns.

### 2.2 Hybrid and remote work

Acme Corp operates a **hybrid-first** model. Employees are expected to be physically present at the assigned office at least **2 days per week** unless their contract specifies fully remote status.

Remote-first contracts are available for senior engineering, design, and writing roles, subject to manager approval and at least 12 months of tenure.

### 2.3 Working from a different country

Employees may work from a country other than their employment country for up to **30 calendar days per year** (cumulative across trips), subject to:

- Notifying their manager and People Operations at least 5 business days in advance
- Tax residency remains in the employment country (longer stays trigger tax implications and are not permitted under this policy)
- Network access from sanctioned countries is prohibited (see IT Security Policy, section 4)
- Working from a non-EU country during paid time off is permitted as personal travel, but accessing company systems outside the 30-day allowance is not — even briefly to "check email"

For stays longer than 30 days, employees must apply for an international assignment through People Operations. Approval is not guaranteed and depends on tax, legal, and immigration constraints.

## 3. Paid time off (PTO)

### 3.1 Annual leave accrual

Full-time employees accrue **2.0 days of PTO per month** (24 days per year). Part-time employees accrue PTO proportional to their contracted hours.

PTO accrual begins on the first day of employment. New hires can take PTO before fully accruing it (up to 5 days in the first 6 months), with the remainder deducted from future accrual.

### 3.2 Carry-over

Employees may carry over up to **5 unused PTO days** to the following calendar year. PTO carried over must be used by March 31; otherwise it is forfeited. There is no cash-out option for unused PTO except at termination.

### 3.3 Approval and notice

PTO requests of:

- **1–4 days**: submit at least 5 business days in advance
- **5+ days**: submit at least 14 business days in advance
- **Emergency PTO** (illness, family emergency): notify manager same-day; written justification within 3 business days

Manager approval is required. PTO that conflicts with critical project milestones or peak coverage periods (e.g., end of fiscal quarter for finance roles) may be deferred.

### 3.4 Public holidays

Acme Corp observes the public holidays of the employee's country of employment. Employees relocating internationally retain their original country's holiday schedule unless they request a change in writing.

## 4. Onboarding (first 30 days)

### 4.1 Week 1 checklist

New hires complete the following in their first week:

1. **IT setup** — laptop pickup or shipment confirmation, MFA enrollment, password vault setup (see IT Security Policy section 2)
2. **HR documents** — signed contract, tax forms, emergency contact, banking details
3. **Security training** — mandatory online module on data classification, phishing awareness, incident reporting (see IT Security Policy section 6)
4. **Benefits enrollment** — health insurance plan selection, retirement contribution rate, optional perks (gym, meal allowance)
5. **Manager 1:1** — at least two 30-minute syncs with direct manager
6. **Buddy assignment** — informal peer mentor from the same team for the first 90 days

The Week 1 checklist must be completed by end of business on Day 5. Incomplete items trigger an automated escalation to People Operations.

### 4.2 First 90 days

The first 90 days are a probationary period during which either party may terminate the employment relationship with **2 weeks' written notice**. Performance feedback is delivered at Day 30, Day 60, and Day 90 by the direct manager.

## 5. Code of conduct (summary)

Acme Corp is committed to a respectful, inclusive workplace. The full Code of Conduct is published as a separate document; key points are summarized here:

- Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind is grounds for immediate termination
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the manager and Compliance within 5 business days of discovery
- External speaking engagements representing Acme Corp require pre-approval from Communications
- Gifts from suppliers, clients, or partners exceeding **EUR 75 in value** must be declared

Reporting channels for misconduct: direct manager, HR partner, or anonymous ethics hotline (number in section 9).

## 6. Compensation and benefits (overview)

Detailed benefits are described in the separate Benefits Guide. Key elements:

- **Base salary** — paid monthly on the 25th, or the preceding business day if 25th falls on a weekend
- **Annual bonus** — discretionary, tied to individual and company performance, paid in March for the previous fiscal year
- **Equity** — RSU grants for eligible roles, vesting on a 4-year schedule with a 1-year cliff
- **Health insurance** — fully employer-paid for the employee; partner and dependents at employee cost (see Benefits Guide)
- **Retirement** — employer match up to 5% of base salary, vesting immediate

## 7. Performance reviews

Performance reviews occur **twice per year** (April and October). Reviews include:

- Self-assessment (employee writes)
- Manager assessment (manager writes)
- Peer feedback (3–5 peers nominated by employee, anonymous)
- Skip-level review (every 18 months)

Calibration occurs at the team level before ratings are finalized. Compensation adjustments are communicated separately, typically 4–6 weeks after review delivery.

---

## 8. Termination and offboarding

### 8.1 Voluntary resignation

Employees must provide written notice per their contract, typically:

- **Individual contributors**: 4 weeks
- **Managers**: 8 weeks
- **Senior leadership (Director+)**: 12 weeks

Unused PTO is paid out at termination at the employee's current daily rate.

### 8.2 Involuntary termination

For-cause termination (gross misconduct, breach of contract) is effective immediately. Without-cause termination follows the notice period in the contract or applicable labor law, whichever is longer.

### 8.3 Final-day checklist

- Return company hardware (laptop, phone, access cards)
- Knowledge transfer document signed by manager
- Final expense submission within 7 days
- Exit interview with HR (optional, encouraged)
